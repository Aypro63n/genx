Privacy policy

FreeAI Studio needs no account and collects no contact details.

1. Local data: history and theme choice are stored only in your browser (local storage). Clearing site data removes them.

2. Generated and uploaded images stay on your device unless you select Publish to Global Gallery.

3. Published items become public. Anyone can view, search, download, and reuse the prompt. Do not publish private or sensitive photos. Published items, including hidden or reported ones, can be reviewed by the moderator for safety. Images you never publish stay only in your browser and cannot be seen by anyone else.

4. Image generation and file hosting use third party Perchance services. Standard technical logs (such as IP based rate limits) may apply at that layer.

5. Contact: use the generator comments or hosting account settings to request removal of a published item.
